## 💡 Common Questions

### Basics
1. "Explain SQL Injection?"
2. "What is XSS?"
3. "Difference between CSRF and XSS?"
4. "Explain OAuth flow?"
5. "What is JWT?"

### Advanced
1. "How do you find vulnerabilities?"
2. "Explain your last hacking project?"
3. "How would you test an API?"
4. "Describe a hard bug you found?"
5. "What's your approach to security?"

### Scenario Questions
1. "You find RCE in production, what do you do?"
2. "Found SSRF, how would you exploit it?"
3. "How do you bypass WAF?"
4. "Explain your recon process?"
5. "How do you avoid duplicates?"

## 💼 Interview Tips

1. **Prepare stories** - Real examples
2. **Know your tools** - Command line fluency
3. **Explain thinking** - Not just answers
4. **Ask questions** - Show interest
5. **Be honest** - Don't fake skills
6. **Show passion** - Love for security
