## ⚖️ Legal Overview

### ❌ Illegal
- Unauthorized access
- Data theft
- Ransom
- DDoS attacks
- Malware distribution

### ✅ Legal
- Authorized penetration testing
- Bug bounty programs
- CTF competitions
- Educational labs
- Your own systems

---

## 💫 Responsible Disclosure

### Process
1. Find vulnerability
2. Contact company privately
3. Give 90 days to fix
4. Don't disclose publicly
5. Get credit/reward

## ⚡ Laws by Country

### India
- IPC Section 66-70
- 5-10 years jail + fine
- IT Act 2000

### USA
- Computer Fraud Act
- 10-20 years prison
- $250,000+ fine

### UK
- Computer Misuse Act
- 15 years prison
- Full criminal record
